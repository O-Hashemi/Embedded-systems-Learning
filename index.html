Project Overview

The Squirtle Smart Garden is an ESP32-powered automated watering system designed to monitor environmental conditions and water plants automatically when needed.

Sensors monitor temperature, humidity, and water levels while a pump delivers water when conditions require it.

This project combines 3D printing, electronics, soldering, programming, and web development into one complete engineering project.

Original Ladybug Design

The project originally began as a ladybug-themed smart garden. While the concept successfully incorporated a water reservoir and irrigation system, the design presented several engineering challenges. Internal space was limited, component placement was difficult, and future maintenance would have been more complicated. These limitations motivated a complete redesign of the project.

Design Evaluation and Redesign

During development, the original design was evaluated to identify weaknesses and opportunities for improvement. Alternative concepts were explored with a focus on increasing internal space, improving accessibility, simplifying water routing, and creating a more reliable final product.

Final Squirtle Smart Garden

The final design combines 3D printing, embedded systems, sensor integration, automated irrigation, and web development into a single engineering project. The ESP32 monitors environmental conditions and controls the watering system while displaying live system data through a custom-built web dashboard.
