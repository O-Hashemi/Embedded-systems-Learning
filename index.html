Project Overview

The Squirtle Smart Garden is an ESP32-powered automated watering system designed to monitor environmental conditions and water plants automatically when needed.

Sensors monitor temperature, humidity, and water levels while a pump delivers water when conditions require it.

This project combines 3D printing, electronics, soldering, programming, and web development into one complete engineering project.

Original Ladybug Design

The project originally began as a ladybug-themed smart garden. The design had a creative appearance, but the internal space was limited. This made it harder to fit the pump, reservoir, wiring, and sensors cleanly inside the model.

Design Evaluation

After testing the original idea, I realized the design would be difficult to maintain and modify. I redesigned the project to improve component access, water routing, stability, and overall reliability.

Final Squirtle Design

The final Squirtle design provides more internal room, better component placement, and a stronger project theme. It combines 3D printing, ESP32 programming, sensors, pump control, and a web dashboard into one complete system.
